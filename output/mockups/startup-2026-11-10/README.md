## Final prompts

### 01-cinema

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one high-fidelity desktop app startup screen mockup for MoarTube Client, a self-hosted video and live-stream management application. This is a visual design concept, no code. Landscape 16:9, crisp pixel-perfect UI. Show the app alone filling the image with a slim native Windows titlebar at top ("MoarTube Client", minimize/maximize/close) and slim app menu ("File", "Edit", "View", "Help"). No desktop wallpaper, no monitor, no perspective.
Direction: CINEMA. Premium nearly-black charcoal canvas with very restrained deep plum ambient light and subtle texture. Elegant editorial composition, center a large simple magenta rounded-rectangle video mark containing two small white right-facing play triangles, then large off-white "MoarTube Client". An oversized extremely subtle abstract looping film-ribbon silhouette sits far behind the main content, cropped at frame edges, adding cinematic character without clutter. Refined, calm, confident video creator software. Brand magenta #cb0c9f is the only strong color. Main lockup at center slightly above midpoint; lots of composed negative space. Below brand, muted readable text "Preparing your workspace" and then a thin indeterminate loading track about 280 pixels wide with a short magenta segment, NOT a percentage. Underneath smaller status "Checking video processing support…" Tiny bottom-left "VIDEO • LIVE • YOURS" and bottom-right "Starting up". All typography legible contemporary sans serif, professional contrast, very polished spacing. No buttons, no dashboard cards, no fake progress numbers, no hardware statistics. A single coherent full-size UI screen, no comparison board, no annotations, no concept title outside UI, no watermark.
